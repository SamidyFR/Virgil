Troubleshooting

"The ads are making sites really hard to navigate!"

An adblocker is pretty much essential when navigating piracy sites. We recommend uBlock Origin.

"I click on a result and it just takes me to a website's homepage"

The site does have the content you are searching for, but it doesn't support Virgil properly.
Just manually search for the content in the site itself, or use another result.

Frequently Asked Questions

"What's Virgil?"

Virgil is a piracy search engine made to help out with beginners and save time instead of checking multiple sites.

"Ehh. The few loved piracy sites are so good that I'm not sure a search engine is really needed."

I can give you a few reasons why a search engine is needed:
1. Helps out with beginners in pirating.
2. Some sites may have the program, but lock it behind a bad file hoster.
3. Some sites may have the program, but it's an outdated version.
4. Some sites may not have the program you want.

"Are you sure these sources are safe?"

Uh.. yeah? Look, here's the deal: you can NEVER be 100% sure a site is safe. they may become malicious one day and start uploading malware.
Which is why we always inform users to be cautious when downloading pirated software.
but with that said, all of the sites indexed are from FMHY, which is regularly updated to catch issues like this.
If an existing site becomes malicious or a site gets added, we update our search engine accordingly ASAP.
